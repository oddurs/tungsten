## What to add

The full CODATA 2022 set, with conventional short names (`c`, `G`, `h`,
`hbar`, `k_B`, `N_A`, `e`, `m_e`…) and long names (`speed of light`).

## Sources

NIST CODATA 2022, physics.nist.gov/cuu/Constants.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Every entry has a `source`
- [x] `w --why c` shows provenance
- [x] Uncertainties stored, ready for v0.6

## 2026-09-23

Done. All 355 CODATA 2022 constants, generated by scripts/data/codata.py from NIST's listing, each with a source and a stored standard uncertainty (or 'exact'), ready for v0.6. Short symbols for the common ones (G, ħ, N_A, k_B, α, ε0, μ0 …); symbols that are already units or constants (c, h, e, F) are shadowed and reachable with --as constant. '--why c' snapshot: c is the unit, defined exactly by BIPM; the CODATA entry 'speed of light in vacuum' is the same number.
